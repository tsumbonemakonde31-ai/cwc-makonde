About Us

A Family Rooted in Christ

CWC Makonde is a ministry under the leadership of Pastor Violet Mathobo.

We are proud to be part of the global CWC family, with branches reaching communities across the world. Here in Makonde, we have built an atmosphere of genuine praise and worship—a spiritual home where everyone is welcomed.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church to grow in, there is a place for you here. Our mission is to equip believers, serve our community, and share the transformative power of Jesus Christ.
